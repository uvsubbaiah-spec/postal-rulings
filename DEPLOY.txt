Postal Rulings: saved-post layout, themes for admins, direct app download

Upload these files to the root of the postal-rulings repo, replacing the current ones:
  index.html  cozy.html  fireside.html  sapphire.html  app-version.json  postal-rulings.apk

Then, in the Android app, pull down at the top of the home page once to reload it.

To switch the Android app to a new design
  Profile (your initial at the top) > App theme > Cozy, Fireside or Sapphire.
  The choice is remembered on that phone. Admin opens the classic app from the Admin card.
