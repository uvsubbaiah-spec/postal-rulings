Postal Rulings: New designs in the Profile menu

Upload index.html to the root of the postal-rulings repo, replacing the current one.
(The other files in this zip are the same as the last delivery; upload them too if you
have not already.)

Then, in the Android app, pull down at the top of the home page once so it loads the
new index.html.

To switch: tap your profile icon at the top > New designs > Sapphire, Fireside or Cozy.
The choice is remembered on that phone. Admin opens the classic app from the Admin card.
