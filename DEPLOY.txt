Postal Rulings: new designs

Upload all four files to the root of the postal-rulings repo, replacing the current ones:
  index.html  cozy.html  fireside.html  sapphire.html

What index.html changes
- Profile > App theme: choosing Cozy, Fireside or Sapphire switches the whole app,
  including the Android app, to that new design. The choice is remembered.
- Shared and notification links (?doc=...) open in the chosen design.
- Admin (the Admin card, or index.html?classic=1) always opens the classic app.
- Choosing Classic in the new design's Profile goes back to the classic app.
